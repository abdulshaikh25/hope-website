In a hazardous area, the motor must carry the right protection and certificate for the zone and gas group. Send us the area classification and the nameplate or duty, and we source a matching motor.

Every motor comes with its test certificate: Indian test-lab certification with PESO approval, or ATEX for export projects, as your site requires. Ask for the documents before you order.

What we supply

  • Flameproof (Ex d) and increased-safety (Ex e) motors
  • Zone 1 and Zone 2, gas groups IIA, IIB and IIC
  • Certificates supplied with every motor
  • Special builds for voltage, speed or mounting

Where it is used

  • Refineries and fuel depots
