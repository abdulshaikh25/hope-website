How every used machine is prepared

The same five steps for every genset and motor before it is listed.

Read our test and warranty policy

  1. Inspect

    Engine, alternator, controls and canopy checked. Hours and serial numbers recorded.

  2. Strip and clean

    Opened up, cleaned, and wear parts measured.

  3. Replace

    Worn parts, filters, oil and coolant replaced.

  4. Load test

    Full-load trial at our yard. You are welcome to watch.

  5. Certify

    Signed test report and written warranty card with the machine.
