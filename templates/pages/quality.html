How we test, and what our warranty covers

Buying used should not be a gamble. This is exactly what happens to a machine before we sell it, and what we put in writing.

Before a genset is listed as Grade A

  1. Inspect

    Engine, alternator, controls, radiator and canopy checked. Engine and alternator serial numbers and hour-meter reading recorded.

  2. Strip and clean

    Opened up and cleaned. Wear parts measured against limits.

  3. Replace

    Worn parts replaced; fresh oil, filters and coolant.

  4. Full-load trial

    Run on a load at our yard. Voltage, frequency, oil pressure and temperature recorded.

  5. Certify

    Test report signed and handed over with a written warranty card.

What is on the test report

  • Stock number, make, model and rating
  • Engine and alternator serial numbers
  • Hour-meter reading and year of manufacture
  • Load applied and test duration
  • Voltage, frequency and current at load
  • Oil pressure, coolant temperature and smoke check
  • Insulation resistance of the alternator or motor
  • Parts replaced during refurbishment
