How hire works

  1. Tell us the load

    Size of load, site, start date and how many hours a day.

  2. We quote

    A matching set from our yard with monthly rent and delivery cost.

  3. Delivery

    Set delivered, placed and connected at your site.

  4. Running

    Our operator can run and look after the set; we handle breakdowns.

    Give 7 days' notice and we collect the set.
