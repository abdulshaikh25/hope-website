Questions buyers ask

Can I see the set running before I pay?

Yes. Every Grade A set gets a full-load trial at our Darukhana yard, and you are welcome to attend. If you can't visit, we send a running video on WhatsApp.

What does the warranty cover?

Refurbished sets come with a written HOPE warranty, {{ site.std_warranty }} as standard, on the engine and alternator. The period is printed on the warranty card. See how we test and warranty.

Can I use a used genset in Maharashtra?

Yes. Sets of 61 kW and above may need an emission control device (RECD) under MPCB rules, depending on your site. We tell you before you buy and can fit it. See RECD compliance.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Yes, across India. We arrange transport, unloading, installation and commissioning.

Will you take my old set in part exchange?

Usually, yes. We inspect it and give you an offer. See sell to us.
