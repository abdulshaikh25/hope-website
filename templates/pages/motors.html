How a used motor is checked

  1. Nameplate and history

    Rating, frame, poles and any rewinding recorded.

  2. Insulation test

    Insulation resistance (and polarisation index on HT motors) measured.

  3. Bearings and rotor

    Bearings, slip rings and brush gear inspected or replaced.

  4. No-load run

    Run on no load with current and vibration noted on the test sheet.
