Make your genset compliant with MPCB rules

The Maharashtra Pollution Control Board requires in-use diesel gensets of 61 kW and above to cut smoke emissions — by fitting a certified Retrofit Emission Control Device (RECD), converting the set, or replacing it. We survey your set and handle the whole job.

Applies to
In-use gensets 61 kW and above
Circular
MPCB, 21 October 2026
Deadline
Short — check your consent

Does this apply to me?

Most likely, if all of these are true:

  • Your site is in Maharashtra
  • You run a diesel genset rated 61 kW (about 75 kVA) or more
  • The set is not a new CPCB IV+ model
  • Your site holds, or needs, MPCB consent to operate

Rules depend on your site and set. Read the circular on the MPCB website or send us your genset nameplate photo and we will tell you what applies.

Common questions

What is an RECD?

A Retrofit Emission Control Device is fitted to an existing genset's exhaust to capture most of the particulate matter (soot) it produces. Only devices certified by an approved testing agency should be used.

How long does fitting take?

Usually a day or two on site once the device is available, depending on the set and exhaust route. We plan it to avoid power cuts at your site.

What happens if I don't comply?

MPCB can take action under your consent conditions, including forfeiting the bank guarantee given with your consent. Check your consent letter and the circular.

Is it cheaper to replace the set?

Sometimes. If your set is old or needs an overhaul, a compliant replacement plus buy-back of the old set can cost less over a few years. We quote both.
