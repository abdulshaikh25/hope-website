Annual maintenance (AMC)

Planned visits, oil and filter changes, test runs and priority breakdown response.

In the workshop

Engines, alternators and motors are opened up, machined and rebuilt by our team and trusted workshop partners in Mumbai.

What an AMC includes

Each AMC is priced for your set's size, hours and location. A typical contract covers:

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance visits
  • Oil, filter and coolant changes (consumables billed at cost)
  • Battery, AVR and AMF panel checks
  • Load test and a written service report each visit
  • Priority response to breakdown calls
  • RECD check and cleaning where fitted

Bought a set from us?

Used sets from HOPE come with on-site troubleshooting support during the warranty period. Add an AMC at purchase to keep the set covered after that.
