How an export order works

  1. Requirement

    Send rating, voltage, frequency (50 or 60 Hz) and destination.

  2. Offer

    Matching units with photos, running video and FOB or CIF price.

  3. Inspection

    Third-party pre-shipment inspection where your country requires it.

  4. Payment

    Advance payment or a letter of credit from your bank.

  5. Shipping

    Packing, export documents and shipment through our freight agent.
